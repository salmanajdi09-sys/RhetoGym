## 🎯 The Problem & Purpose

Prepared presentations and real-time, impromptu communication require fundamentally different skills. While millions suffer from speech anxiety, practicing in front of a live audience is intimidating. 

**RhetoGym** bridges this gap by creating an accessible, zero-friction space to practice *thinking on your feet*. It provides instantaneous speech prompts, dynamic timing parameters, and private post-session analysis so users can catch their habits, track pacing, and build confidence over time.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 💡 **Dynamic Prompt Engine:** Instant generator for impromptu speaking, debate topics, and storytelling exercises in both **English and French**.
* ⏱️ **Custom Countdown Timers:** Dedicated preparation and speaking countdowns designed to simulate real-world time pressure.
* 📊 **Self-Reflection & Analytics:** Review transcripts, analyze pace, catch filler words, add timestamped notes, and monitor confidence trends over time in your **Round History**.
* 🔒 **100% Free & Zero Friction:** No account creation, passwords, or paywalls required.
